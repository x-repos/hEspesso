cobalt powder (Aldrich Chemical, 99.9%, <150 mm) were
mixed in different proportions: 2MgeCo, 3MgeCo, and
7MgeCo.Afterfivecleaningcyclesofthevials,consistingof

withhydrogen(99.999%).Aplanetarymill(FritschP7,600rpm)
wasusedforthereactivemillingduring12h,usingaball-to-
powderweightratioof40:1andhydrogenpressureof3MPa.
